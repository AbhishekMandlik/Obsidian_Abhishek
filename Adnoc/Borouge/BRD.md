Borouge is polyolefins producer with integrated operation spanning production, supply chain planning, logistics, sales, and global market engagement.

Demand Planning AI: short-term demand forecasting.  Current process relies on Quintiq statistical baseline and planner adjustments, while many commercial and market signals are interpreted manually.

| Business Objective                             | Outcome Expected from MVP/ Near-term Scale-up                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Improve short-term forecast quality            | Introduce an AI demand forecast that outperforms or clarifies current baseline/consensus behavior in important markets/materials.                      |
| Reduce manual dependency                       | Shift from planner-heavy driver interpretation to a repeatable feature-driven forecasting pipeline.                                                    |
| Increase forecast transparency                 | Provide planner-facing KPI outputs such as weighted accuracy, bias, and FVA with clear lineage and versioning.                                         |
| Use internal + external signals systematically | Engineer the most relevant internal and external drivers into a governed monthly forecasting process.                                                  |
| Create a scalable foundation                   | Enable later expansion to broader driver expansion, Material & Country mix, and deeper platform integration without redesigning the core architecture. |
**Why the AI-based demand forecasting solution is valuable for Borouge**:

- **More accurate and intelligent forecasts**: Instead of relying mainly on historical sales and manual judgment, the model uses multiple business and external drivers to predict demand more effectively.
- **Higher confidence in forecasts**: Planners can trust the forecast more because it is based on data-driven insights and measurable performance.
- **Better visibility of exceptions and risks**: The solution can highlight unusual demand patterns, changes, or potential issues earlier, helping planners focus on important exceptions. 
- **Reduced manual effort**: It automates much of the analysis that planners currently perform manually, saving time and improving consistency. 
- **Clearer KPIs and actionable outputs**: Users receive meaningful metrics such as forecast accuracy, bias, and forecast value add (FVA), along with planner-friendly outputs that support decision-making.
- **Scalable foundation for the future**: The solution is designed so that more data sources, markets, products, and advanced capabilities can be added later without redesigning the entire system.
